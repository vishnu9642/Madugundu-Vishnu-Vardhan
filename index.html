01 ABOUT ME
01 / 02

Building things with purpose.

I’m Vishnu Vardhan, a technology enthusiast passionate about embedded systems, robotics, electronics, automation, and emerging technologies.

With a background in Electrical and Electronics Engineering, I developed a strong interest in understanding how hardware, electronics, sensors, microcontrollers, and control systems come together to create intelligent and automated solutions. My curiosity has led me to explore embedded technologies, robotic systems, IoT devices, and hardware-software integration.

I enjoy working with technologies such as microcontrollers, Arduino, ESP32, sensors, actuators, communication modules, embedded C, IoT, and robotics platforms. I’m particularly interested in building practical systems where electronic components can sense their surroundings, process information, and perform meaningful actions.
